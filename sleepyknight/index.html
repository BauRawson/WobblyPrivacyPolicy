Privacy Policy for SleepyKnight

Last updated: August 19, 2026

SleepyKnight respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you play the game and how it is used.

Information We Collect

SleepyKnight does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, age, or address.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or other information that directly identifies you.

Analytics

SleepyKnight uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is used and improve the player experience. GameAnalytics may collect limited gameplay and technical information, such as gameplay events, device and browser information, operating system, approximate location derived from an IP address, and identifiers used for analytics.

We use this information to understand general gameplay behavior, diagnose technical issues, and improve the game. GameAnalytics processes information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

SleepyKnight does not currently display advertising. If the game is published on Poki or another platform that serves advertising in the future, that platform or its advertising partners may process information according to their own privacy policies and may use cookies or similar technologies.

Where required, the platform may present consent or other privacy controls. We will update this policy before or when advertising is introduced.

Children's Privacy

SleepyKnight does not intentionally ask players to provide personal information.

If you believe personal information from a child has been collected through the game, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Data Sharing

We do not sell players' personal information.

Limited information may be processed by GameAnalytics and, if advertising is introduced, by the relevant game platform or advertising providers, subject to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features, analytics, advertising, or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
